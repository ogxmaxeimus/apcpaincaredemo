Meet the physician

Sonia Pasi, MD

Interventional pain specialist and neurologist

Dr. Pasi completed fellowship training in interventional pain management at Duke University Medical Center. She looks for the source of pain, then builds a plan around that person — not a standard protocol.

Care can include medication, image-guided procedures, physical therapy, psychology support, acupuncture, and changes in daily habits. She works with referring physicians and performs procedures together with her assistant, so the same hands stay on the case.

  • Fellowship trained in interventional pain management at Duke
  • Evaluates pain as both a neurologist and a pain physician
  • Spine, joint, nerve, and injury care under one plan
  • In-house physical therapy, psychology, and acupuncture

What to expect

The first visit is a conversation and an exam.

Dr. Pasi reviews how the pain started, what imaging shows, and how it is changing daily life. You leave with an explanation and a recommendation — not a stack of unexplained orders.
