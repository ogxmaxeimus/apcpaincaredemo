The physician

Sonia Pasi, MD

Interventional pain specialist and neurologist. Fellowship trained at Duke University Medical Center.

Dr. Pasi is the lead physician at Advanced Pain Consultants. She is trained in advanced interventional techniques and has taken part in clinical studies related to chronic pain.

Her approach combines medication when it is appropriate, minimally invasive procedures, physical therapy, psychotherapy, acupuncture, and changes in diet and daily life. The goal is an active life with less pain.

She and her assistant perform procedures themselves. Referring doctors stay in the loop, because most patients arrive with a story that already includes other clinicians, imaging, and treatments that did not hold.

  • Fellowship, interventional pain management, Duke University Medical Center
  • Neurologic evaluation alongside pain procedures
  • Spine, joint, nerve, headache, and injury-related pain
  • Care coordinated with therapy, psychology, and acupuncture
