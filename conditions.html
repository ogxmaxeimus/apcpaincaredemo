Spine

Back and neck

Chronic back pain

Pain that stays in the low back or spreads into the hip or leg, including pain from discs and arthritis.

Chronic neck pain

Stiffness, cracking, or pain that limits turning the head, often tied to joints or nerves in the neck.

Disc injuries

Bulging or herniated discs that irritate a nearby nerve.

Compression fractures

Spine fractures, often related to bone weakness, that need a careful diagnosis before any procedure.

Nerves

Nerve pain and headache

Neuropathic pain

Burning, shooting, or electric pain from a nerve that is injured or irritated.

Complex regional pain

Pain after an injury that is out of proportion to the original event, sometimes with color or swelling changes.

Phantom limb pain

Pain felt in a limb that is no longer there.

Post-stroke pain

Nerve pain that can follow a stroke and needs neurologic context.

Migraines and headaches

Headache patterns that may involve nerves, joints, or muscle, including occipital nerve pain.

Joints and injuries

After an accident, a sport, or a surgery

Arthritis and joint pain

Hips, shoulders, knees, and the small joints of the spine.

Auto accident injuries

Pain after a crash, including cases that involve an attorney, insurer, or lien.

Work-related injuries

Care that can fit a workers’ compensation claim, with the records that claim needs.

Sports injuries

Joint and soft-tissue pain that is keeping you out of activity.

Post-surgical pain

Recovery after an operation, when the expected course of pain is harder than it should be.

Broader pain

When pain has more than one driver

Fibromyalgia

Widespread pain with fatigue and sleep trouble, treated as a whole-person problem.

Cancer pain

Chronic pain related to cancer or its treatment, coordinated with the rest of your physicians.
