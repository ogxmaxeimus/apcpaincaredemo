New patients

Four steps, then a plan you understand.

The first visit is an examination, an explanation, and a recommendation. Bring the records that let Dr. Pasi start from what is already known.

01

Initial examination

Your pain, health history, and goals are the starting point. Care is shaped around that, not a template.

02

Report of findings

Dr. Pasi explains the condition and how it can affect the rest of your health.

03

Treatment recommendation

You will hear which options fit, and how often treatment may be needed. That can include therapy, psychology, acupuncture, exercise, or diet changes.

04

Follow-through

Medication, when used, is monitored. Procedures are performed by Dr. Pasi and her assistant.

What to bring

The visit goes faster with the paperwork in hand.

  • Completed patient forms
  • Photo ID
  • Insurance card
  • Referral from your physician, if you have one
  • Imaging and other diagnostic studies
  • A list of medications
  • Medical records, if you have them
  • For a work claim: case number, adjuster, attorney, and case manager contacts
