The practice

A Raleigh clinic built around one physician.

Advanced Pain Consultants, PA treats chronic pain from the spine, joints, nerves, and injuries. The aim is lasting relief and a daily life that feels like yours again.

Chronic pain reaches work, sleep, mood, and the people around you. Dr. Sonia Pasi listens for that full picture, then looks for the source rather than only quieting a symptom.

She has guided hundreds of patients through pain care, and she coordinates with the physicians who refer them. Plans are explained in plain language so you know what is being treated and why.

When recovery needs more than a procedure, the office can include physical therapy, psychology support, and acupuncture — arranged as part of the same plan.
