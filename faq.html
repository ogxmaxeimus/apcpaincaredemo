FAQ

Questions people ask before they call.

If the answer you need is not here, the front desk can take it at (919) 510-7901.

Where is the office, and when is it open?

3200 Blue Ridge Road, Suite 216, Raleigh, NC 27612. Monday through Thursday, 8:30am to 4:30pm. Friday, 9:00am to 12:30pm. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

Do I need a referral?

Some plans require one. If you have a referral, bring it. Physicians can also send patients through the practice referral page. Call if you are not sure what your plan requires.

What happens at the first visit?

Dr. Pasi examines you, explains the findings, and recommends a plan. That plan may include a procedure, medication, therapy, psychology support, acupuncture, or changes in daily habits.

What should I bring?

Photo ID, insurance card, a medication list, completed forms, and any imaging or records. Injury and workers’ compensation visits also need the claim contacts.

Which insurance do you accept?

The clinic lists Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, Coventry, Gateway, Medicare, Medcost, TriCare, United Healthcare, VA health care, auto insurance, and workers’ compensation. Coverage still depends on your specific plan.

Do you treat pain after a car accident or a work injury?

Yes. The practice evaluates auto accident and work-related injuries and can work with the documentation those cases require.

Is therapy available in the office?

Physical therapy, psychology support, and acupuncture are offered in-house so recovery is not split across unrelated clinics.

Who performs procedures?

Dr. Pasi and her assistant perform procedures themselves.

How do I request an appointment?

Call (919) 510-7901, email admin@apcpaincare.com, or use the appointment form. The form opens an email to the clinic so the request is not stored on this website.
